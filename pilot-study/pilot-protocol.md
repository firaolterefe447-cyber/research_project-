# Pilot Study Protocol

## Purpose

Test readability, completion time, item clarity, response options, observation practicality, and interview prompts before full data collection.

## Pilot Sample

Use 5–8 participants representing, to the extent possible, the target population. Include at least one instructor if availability permits. Pilot participants will not be included in the main sample when that could bias responses.

## Procedure

1. Give participants the questionnaire or interview guide.
2. Ask whether each item was clear and whether any relevant topic was missing.
3. Record completion time.
4. Conduct one practice classroom observation.
5. Group members compare coding decisions using three anonymized textual responses.
6. Revise item wording, scales, or instructions without changing the approved research objectives.
7. Document every revision and the reason for it.

## Evaluation Questions

- Did participants understand each item without explanation?
- Did the response scale provide suitable choices?
- Did the questionnaire take less than approximately 15 minutes?
- Did any item collect unnecessary personal information?
- Were the interview prompts neutral and non-leading?
- Did the observation checklist use observable facts rather than opinions?
- Did coders apply the codebook consistently?
