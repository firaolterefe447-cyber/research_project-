# Semi-Structured Interview Guide

## Participants

Select 5–10 instructors and, if appropriate, a smaller purposive sample of students.

## Opening

The purpose of this interview is to understand experiences with attendance management and views about digital alternatives. Participation is voluntary. The interview can be stopped at any time. Ask permission before recording.

## Core Questions

1. Please describe the attendance process normally used in the courses you attend or teach.
2. What difficulties have you observed with the manual process?
3. Approximately how much time does attendance take at the beginning or end of a session?
4. How does attendance management affect teaching or learning time?
5. What errors, disputes, or attendance-fraud concerns occur?
6. How is attendance information stored and corrected?
7. What digital attendance technologies are you familiar with?
8. What potential benefits do you see in a digital system?
9. What barriers could prevent successful use?
10. How could privacy, affordability, internet access, accessibility, and technical support be addressed?
11. What safeguards would make a digital system acceptable to students and instructors?
12. What recommendation would you give the university?

## Probes

- Can you give an example?
- What happened next?
- Why do you think that occurred?
- Is that a common problem or an isolated case?

## Closing

Ask whether the participant wishes to add any information. Explain how their data will be anonymized and stored.
