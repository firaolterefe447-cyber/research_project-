## 1. Introduction and Background

Addis Ababa University (AAU) plays a significant role in higher education, research, and technological development in Ethiopia. As educational institutions increasingly adopt digital technologies, improving the efficiency of academic activities has become essential.

Traditional attendance management methods, such as calling students' names or using paper-based attendance sheets, may consume instructional time, produce inaccurate records, and increase administrative workloads. Digital attendance systems, including QR-code-based and RFID-based technologies, offer potential solutions by improving the speed, accuracy, and efficiency of attendance recording.

## 2. Statement of the Problem

Manual attendance management may create challenges such as instructional time loss, attendance fraud, record inaccuracies, and difficulties in managing student information. These problems can be particularly significant in large university classrooms.

Although digital attendance systems may address these limitations, their adoption requires consideration of infrastructure, cost, accessibility, and user acceptance. Therefore, this study aims to investigate existing attendance management challenges at Addis Ababa University and assess the potential benefits of adopting digital alternatives.

## 3. Research Objectives

### Main Objective

To assess the challenges of manual attendance management and the potential benefits of digital attendance systems at Addis Ababa University.

### Specific Objectives

1. Identify the major challenges of manual attendance recording.
2. Examine the perceived impact of manual attendance on instructional time and academic efficiency.
3. Assess students' and instructors' acceptance of digital attendance systems.
4. Identify barriers to implementation and recommend practical solutions.

## 4. Research Methodology

The study will employ a descriptive cross-sectional design using both quantitative and qualitative approaches. Data will be collected from approximately 60–100 students and 5–10 instructors at selected AAU departments through questionnaires, interviews, and classroom observations.

Convenience and purposive sampling techniques will be applied. Quantitative data will be analyzed using descriptive statistics, while qualitative responses will be examined thematically.

## 5. Significance of the Study

The research is expected to provide useful recommendations for improving attendance management and reducing unnecessary instructional interruptions. Its findings may assist Addis Ababa University in:

- Identifying appropriate digital attendance solutions.
- Improving administrative efficiency.
- Supporting technology-based academic services.
- Establishing a foundation for future research and development.

## 6. Expected Outcomes and Continuity

The study is expected to:

- Identify the main limitations of manual attendance systems.
- Evaluate perceptions of digital attendance alternatives.
- Recommend feasible improvements.

Future research could involve developing and testing a QR-code-based attendance system to measure time savings, accuracy, accessibility, and user satisfaction.

## 7. Conclusion

This research aims to contribute to more efficient academic administration at Addis Ababa University by investigating manual attendance challenges and exploring practical digital alternatives. Its findings could support evidence-based decisions and encourage further technological improvements in university attendance management.
